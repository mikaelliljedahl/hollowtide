# Hollowtide — vision

Hollowtide is a 2D metroidvania about finding your way through an interconnected underground
world. It has no sequence of separate levels where each episode ends and is left behind.
The player moves around a shared map, returning to familiar rooms and discovering shortcuts
when new capabilities change what is possible.

## Design pillar

**Abilities drive progress.** A new ability should change the interpretation of places the player
has already seen. Environments must therefore contain clear but silent opportunities for return visits,
and progress should feel like understanding rather than a series of completed levels.

**Isolation and atmosphere are central.** Caves, light, distance, sound and movement build
atmosphere. The world communicates through spatial form and changes in the environment, not through lengthy
explanations.

**The environment teaches.** A niche, a passage, an obstacle or a point of light should be able to show
what matters without text on the screen. Game feel — jumping, momentum, aim and response —
must be credible before new content is placed on top of it.

**The protagonist is built up.** The main character is her own female figure without armor. She
starts out vulnerable in appearance and gains equipment and abilities during exploration.
Visual consistency and vulnerability are more important than ready-made hero status from the start.

## This is not Hollowtide

- Not a game with separate levels, score screens that replace a world or progress
with progress primarily measured in experience points.
- Not a text-driven adventure with dialogue, cutscenes or tutorial pop-ups explaining what the environment can show.
- Not an action game where content is built before movement, jumping and aiming feel right.
- Not a story about an already fully equipped hero.
- Not a copy of any other game's music, names or visual assets; everything is Hollowtide's own expression.

Details and binding values can be found in [game-feel.md](game-feel.md),
[asset-contract.md](asset-contract.md) and [code-contract.md](code-contract.md).
## Originality and references

Other games may inform broad genre conventions only. Hollowtide must not copy their names, assets, UI, or distinctive mechanics. Hollowtide music is original: never ship, sample, remix, or transcribe existing music.
